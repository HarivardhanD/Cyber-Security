# SOC L1 Alert Triage

- AN `ALERT` is core concept and detecting it or ignoring it decides the fate of your company

- `EVENTS` and `ALERTS`
    - `EVENTS` --> Means someone logging in , launching applications etc
    - These `EVENTS` are captured in the form of logs for survivalence and sent to `SIEM`
    - Then in `SIEM` it is either seen as normal log or an `ALERT`

- `soc L1` --> gets many alerts and he distinguishes btw good and bad and sends to L2
- `SOC L2` --> analyses and remidises alert sent by L1
- `SOC ENGINEER` --> Ensure the alerts contain enough information required for efficient alert triage
- `SOC MANAGER` --> Track speed and quality of alert triage to ensure that real attacks won't be missed

- ALERT PROPERTIES :
    - ALERT TIME
    - ALERT NAME
    - ALERT SEVERITY
    - ALERT STATUS
    - ALERT VERDICT
    - ALERT ASSIGNEE
    - ALERT DESCRIPTION
    - ALERT FIELDS

- Now alert prioritization :
    - Picking the right `ALERT` 

- ALERT TRIAGE IS INFORMATION ABOUT THE ALERT
