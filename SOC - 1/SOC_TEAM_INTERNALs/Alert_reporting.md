# SOC ALERT REPORTING

- If alert is true positive we need to report it to L2 with proper documentaion of the alert 
- ALERT ESCALATION --> Sending true positive alert to L2 for further investigation\

- SOC ALERT Report format has `5 W`:
    - WHO --> WHO LOGGED IN SUSPICIOUSLY
    - WHAT --> WHAT ACTIONS WERE PERFORMED BY THREAT ACTOR
    - WHEN --> WHEN DID THIS ALL START
    - WHERE --> WHICH DEVICE OR IP WERE INVOLVED
    - WHY --> WHY IS THIS SUS AND WHY THIS HAPPENED
