# SOC Metrics and Objectives

- The main goal of SOC is to protect the CIA of the company's asset.

- `Triage` in a Security Operations Center (SOC) is the initial, critical process of receiving, analyzing, validating, and prioritizing security alerts to distinguish true threats from false positives

- Metric 1 : `CORE METRIC`
    - `ALERT COUNTS` --> TOTAL COUNT OF ALERT RECEIVED
    - `FALSE POSITIVE RATE` --> TOTAL FALSE POSITIVE ALERTS
    - `ALERT ESCALATION RATE` --> TOTAL ESCALATED ALERT
    - `THREAT DETECTION RATE` --> DETECTED THREATS

- `TRIAGE METRICS`
    - It is imp to respond to alert and take necessary actions needed.
    - `SOC TEAM AVAILABILITY` --> SOC team is available 24/7 or 8/5(mon to fri)
    - `MEAN TIME TO DETECT` --> 5 MINUTES --> Average time between the attack and its detection by SOC tools
    - `MEAN TIME TO ACKNOWLEDGE` --> 10 minutes	 --> Time for L1 analysts to start triage of the new alert
    - `MEAN TIME TO RESPOND (MTTR)`--> 60 MIN --> Time by which SOC stops the breach from spreading
